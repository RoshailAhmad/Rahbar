## q08: What is the policy on faculty starting a technical start-up?

**Answer:** The policy on faculty starting a technical start-up is that it must be based on research work conducted at NUCES, involve a full-time faculty member, and be commercialized through a licensing agreement with the university. The policy also outlines the objectives, eligibility criteria, evaluation criteria, and reporting requirements.

A student can repeat a course if that course has not been passed or the student desires to improve his/her grade. A student must repeat all failed courses.
